Featured Project

AgriLink Animal Nutrition
Sales, Inventory & Supply Chain Analytics

An integrated Power BI solution designed to provide end-to-end visibility across Sales Performance, Inventory Control, Import Operations, Procurement, Supplier Performance, Shipments, and Financial Obligations.

Project Scope

An integrated Power BI solution developed to provide end-to-end visibility across sales performance, inventory management, procurement, import operations, shipments, and supplier activities.

01 — Sales Performance Dashboard

This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of the company's sales performance during 2026. It tracks key performance indicators such as total sales value, quantity sold in tons, gross profit, customer performance, regional performance, and product performance. The interactive product images on the left side also work as slicers, allowing users to select a specific product and dynamically filter the entire dashboard. The dashboard helps management identify top-performing customers, analyze product demand, monitor sales trends, and understand the main drivers of revenue.

02 — Inventory Control Dashboard

This dashboard focuses on inventory visibility and stock management. It monitors current stock levels, inventory value, stock movement, days in stock, and inventory turnover. A Reorder Point mechanism is also included to identify products that are approaching critical stock levels. The dashboard also analyzes supplier lead times and product consumption rates to support proactive replenishment planning. By comparing how quickly each product is consumed with the expected supplier lead time, the system helps estimate when a replenishment order should be placed before inventory reaches a critical level. This allows the business to anticipate future stock requirements, reduce stockout risks, and ensure that purchase orders are initiated early enough to cover the supplier's lead time. The dashboard also analyzes how long each product remains in inventory, supporting better inventory turnover and working capital management.

03 — Import, Suppliers & Procurement Dashboard

This dashboard provides visibility into the company's international procurement and import operations. It tracks incoming shipments, imported quantities, supplier lead times, product targets, target achievement, pending shipments, and supplier performance. The dashboard also monitors supplier invoices, payment status, payment due dates, and financial obligations. By connecting procurement data with shipment information, the dashboard helps management understand what has already arrived, what is currently in transit, which products are behind target, and which financial payments require attention.

Project 02

Sales & Inventory Analytics (2024–2026)

A Power BI solution designed to analyze three years of sales performance and inventory movement. The project enables management to compare annual performance, monitor product demand, identify top customers, evaluate inventory turnover, detect slow-moving products, and optimize replenishment decisions.

Business Objective

Build an interactive reporting system that allows decision makers to monitor sales growth across three years while maintaining healthy inventory levels and reducing stock shortages.

01 — Sales Analysis Dashboard

This dashboard compares sales performance across 2024, 2025, and 2026. Users can analyze monthly sales trends, compare yearly growth, identify top-selling products, discover the highest-value customers, evaluate regional performance, and determine which products perform best during each month. Interactive filters allow users to switch between years, regions, and products instantly, providing a complete understanding of business performance.

02 — Inventory Analysis Dashboard

This dashboard focuses on inventory performance over a three-year period. It monitors inventory levels, inventory turnover, stock aging, slow-moving products, fast-moving products, inventory availability, and reorder point status. These insights help management optimize inventory, reduce excess stock, prevent stock shortages, and improve purchasing decisions based on product demand.
